About

I'm Mohammed Shafick, a software developer driven by curiosity and a desire to understand what actually happens behind the scenes.

For me, development isn't just about writing code that works. It's about understanding why it works, how different pieces fit together, and what happens beneath the surface. I enjoy exploring the internals of systems, solving challenging problems, experimenting with ideas, and turning concepts into practical solutions.

I'm constantly learning, questioning assumptions, and looking beyond the obvious to understand the engineering behind the software we use every day. Whether I'm debugging a complex issue, exploring a new technology, or building something from scratch, I believe there's always more to understand.

I'm not someone who claims to know everything. I'm a developer who keeps asking questions, breaking things, learning from mistakes, and building better solutions along the way.

For me, coding is more than writing instructions for a machine. It's about understanding the system, solving the problem, and knowing why the solution works.
